# Mixed-signal board coverage

This inventory tracks every system profile in `configs/systems/*.yaml` (including demos,
fixtures, and multiple profiles for the same physical board). A profile is covered only when
a board-specific test solves an analog device/netlist, routes it to a modeled board endpoint,
and asserts the result. Endpoints include an ADC conversion or a voltage-thresholded digital
input on profiles without an ADC or without an externally routable ADC input. A GPIO-only proof
does not imply ADC support. Generic chip bus tests, analog output traces without a board endpoint,
and device-only unit tests do not count.

Current inventory: **103 top-level profiles; 103 have end-to-end analog coverage; 0 have no end-to-end analog coverage.** The 26 exact-part profiles in `configs/systems/stm32-catalog/` are also covered (see [STM32 catalog profiles](#stm32-catalog-profiles)). Of the 245 imported profiles in `configs/systems/onboarding/`, 139 are covered end to end through a GPIO input (12 of them also through every ADC instance they declare, and 22 simavr AVR parts also through a firmware conversion on their CPU-owned ADC), and 106 declare neither a GPIO input nor a convertible ADC (see [Onboarding profiles](#onboarding-profiles)). Each round selects the next uncovered profile and extends shared device, netlist, or routing support where needed. Coverage through GPIO thresholds does not imply ADC support.

## STM32 catalog profiles

All 26 exact-part smoke profiles in `configs/systems/stm32-catalog/*.yaml` (STM32F103 C/R/T x4/x6 including the `h` BGA variants, STM32F401 C/R/V xB–xE, STM32F407 V/Z/I xE/xG) are covered by one descriptor-driven proof: [`cosim_stm32_catalog_analog.rs`](../crates/core/tests/cosim_stm32_catalog_analog.rs)::`every_stm32_catalog_profile_routes_ntc_and_diode_netlists_into_adc1`. For each part it reads ADC1's base, register profile (F1 or F4) and clock gate from the chip descriptor, then routes the generic Beta NTC divider to PA0 and the unrelated Shockley diode to the package's highest-numbered bonded ADC1 pad (PB1/IN9 on 36- and 48-pin packages, PC5/IN15 on 64-pin and larger). Both solved voltages are checked against independent references and read back through a firmware-style one-shot conversion. On packages without PC0, a route to `board.analog.pc0_volts` must fail at binding time. The test enumerates the directory, so a new catalog profile is covered without editing the test, and the asserted profile count flags it for this inventory.

## Onboarding profiles

The 245 imported profiles in `configs/systems/onboarding/*.yaml` are inventoried by one descriptor-driven proof: [`cosim_onboarding_analog.rs`](../crates/core/tests/cosim_onboarding_analog.rs)::`every_onboarding_profile_with_a_convertible_adc_routes_ntc_and_diode_into_each_instance`. For each profile it reads every non-stub ADC from the chip descriptor, picks the converter family from the peripheral type and `config.profile`, routes the generic Beta NTC divider and the unrelated Shockley diode to `adc.<id>.5_volts` / `adc.<id>.6_volts`, checks both solved voltages against independent references, and reads them back through that family's firmware register sequence. NTC codes must fall monotonically from -10 °C to 80 °C.

### GPIO inputs

[`cosim_onboarding_gpio_analog.rs`](../crates/core/tests/cosim_onboarding_gpio_analog.rs)::`every_onboarding_profile_with_a_gpio_input_takes_routed_ntc_and_diode_levels` covers every onboarding profile whose bus models a GPIO input. The port is the first peripheral the built bus reports as a GPIO input. The same Beta NTC divider and Shockley diode are routed to `board.gpio_in.<port>.0` / `.1` and their solved voltages are checked against independent references. The NTC pin has to follow a -10 → 25 → 80 → 25 → -10 °C sweep with Schmitt hysteresis, and the diode pin has to stay below VIL. Both levels are read back through `Peripheral::read_gpio_input` and through a firmware load from the port's input register. Thresholds are 0.3/0.7 × 3.3 V simulation assumptions, because no import records any. This is GPIO threshold coverage, not ADC support.

### simavr AVR profiles

The simavr imports used to carry `peripherals: []`. [`cosim_onboarding_avr_analog.rs`](../crates/core/tests/cosim_onboarding_avr_analog.rs)::`every_simavr_profile_on_a_modelled_avr_core_converts_ntc_and_diode_in_firmware` covers the parts whose register map is one LabWired's AVR cores run. The map was checked against each part's avr-libc 2.0.0 `io*.h`: PINx/DDRx/PORTx, ADCL/ADCH 0x78/0x79, ADCSRA 0x7A, ADCSRB 0x7B, ADMUX 0x7C. Those descriptors now declare the same `avr_gpio` port mirrors, `avr_adc` input window and `analog_pins` as the in-tree ATmega328P/1281/2560 descriptors. The classic ATmega8/16/32 run on their own I/O-space cores (`AvrModel::Atmega8/16/32`): ADCL/ADCH 0x24/0x25, ADCSRA 0x26, ADMUX 0x27, ports ascending from 0x30, SRAM from 0x60, and the `avr_adc` window at 0x1_0060. They have no ADCSRB, so the test converts with STS ADMUX/ADCSRA there, and it also checks the internal 2.56 V reference (REFS = 11) and the 1.30 V (ATmega8, MUX 0x0E) or 1.22 V (16/32, MUX 0x1E) bandgap. The ATmega164/324/644/1284P run on the port-A cores (`AvrModel::Atmega164/324/644/1284`): avr-libc `iomxx4.h`/`iom324p.h`/`iom644.h`/`iom1284p.h` place every 328P register at the 328P's address and add PINA/DDRA/PORTA at 0x20..0x22. Their ADC is ADC0..7 on PA0..PA7 with MUX4:0, so the test also checks REFS = 10 (internal 1.1 V), REFS = 11 (internal 2.56 V), the MUX 0x1E bandgap, and that the 328P's bandgap code 0x0E reads 0 there. The ATmega128RFA1/128RFR2 run on the RF core (`AvrModel::Atmega128Rf`): avr-libc `iom128rfa1.h`/`iom128rfr2.h` keep the 1281's ports A..G and ADC registers (0x100..0x10B is empty, no ports H..L), with 16 KB SRAM to 0x41FF. Their ADC is powered from the internal 1.8 V AVDD regulator (datasheets 8266F and 8393C, chapter 27), so codes are against 1.8 V and the NTC divider clips at 0x3FF below 0 °C. The test also checks REFS = 10 (internal 1.5 V), REFS = 11 (internal 1.6 V), the 1.2 V VBG on MUX 0x1E, AVSS on 0x1F, and a firmware poll of ADCSRB.AVDDOK/REFOK (0 before ADEN, 1 after). The ATtiny25/45/85 run on the ATtiny x5 cores (`AvrModel::Attiny25/45/85`): avr-libc `iotn25.h`/`iotn45.h`/`iotn85.h` (`iotnx5.h`) keep ADCL..ADMUX at the classic 0x24..0x27 and PINB/DDRB/PORTB at 0x36..0x38 (port B is the only port), with SRAM from 0x60 to RAMEND 0xDF/0x15F/0x25F and PUD in MCUCR bit 6. Their ADC is ADC0..3 on PB5, PB2, PB4, PB3 (datasheet 2586Q Table 17-4), and ADMUX bit 4 is REFS2, not MUX4. So the test also checks REFS2:0 = 010 (internal 1.1 V) and 110 (internal 2.56 V), the 1.1 V bandgap on MUX 0x0C, and that 0x04 (differential), 0x0D (GND), 0x0E and 0x0F (temperature sensor, not modelled) read 0. GPIO thresholds are VIL 0.3 / VIH 0.6 VCC (Table 21-1, VCC 2.4..5.5 V). The ATtiny24/44/84 run on the ATtiny x4 cores (`AvrModel::Attiny24/44/84`): avr-libc `iotn24.h`/`iotn44.h`/`iotn84.h` (`iotnx4.h`) keep ADCSRB..ADMUX at 0x23..0x27, add PINA/DDRA/PORTA at 0x39..0x3B beside port B at 0x36..0x38, and share the x5 parts' SRAM, flash and MCUCR.PUD. Their ADC is ADC0..7 on PA0..PA7 (datasheet 8183F Table 16-4) with a six-bit MUX5:0 in ADMUX, so ADLAR moves to ADCSRB bit 4. The test checks REFS = 10 (internal 1.1 V), the 1.1 V bandgap on MUX 0x21, that 0x08, 0x0C, 0x1E, 0x20 (AGND) and 0x22 (temperature sensor, not modelled) read 0, and that firmware setting ADCSRB.ADLAR reads a left-adjusted diode code. Every conversion is read back with LDS ADCL/ADCH. The generic Beta NTC divider routes to the lowest ADC pad and the Shockley diode to the highest, through `board.analog.<pad>_volts`. The CPU core the descriptor name selects converts both with an STS ADCSRB/ADMUX/ADCSRA sequence. PK7/ADC15 on the 100-pin megas exercises ADCSRB.MUX5. Codes must equal `mV * 1024 / AVcc` (5 V, or 1.8 V AVDD on the RF parts), and the NTC code must fall monotonically from -10 °C to 80 °C wherever it is below full scale.

AVcc is the core's fixed 5 V reference, and the 0.3/0.6 VCC GPIO thresholds (0.2/0.6 VCC on the classic ATmega8/16/32) are the datasheet DC limits. The classic cores model only the map, the ports and the ADC; their timers, USART, SPI and TWI registers are stored only, and they take no interrupts. The port-A cores share the 328P's timers, USART0, SPI and TWI and enter their own vector slots, but INTn/PCINT, output-compare pads and USART1 are stored only. RAM size and SP reset follow the selected core, not the smaller parts' datasheets (ATmega48: 512 B). That does not affect the conversion path tested here.
