# nrf54l05 (Nordic nRF54L family)

**Scope:** executable Cortex-M33 application-core subset, sim-validated.
Not full SoC or physical board support.

- Correct RRAM/SRAM sizes and core MMIO addresses from Zephyr Nordic DTS.
- Covered: UARTE20 EasyDMA, GPIO P0/P1/P2, TIMER10 and SysTick.
- Executed: part-specific banner, memory-geometry banner, GPIO P2.09 edges,
  expected stop reason.
- The test's LED and UART wiring use a **synthetic** board, not a real DK pinout.
- Not qualified: radio/BLE, TrustZone, crypto, full SoC peripherals,
  real-silicon register parity.
