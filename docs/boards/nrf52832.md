# nRF52832 — nRF52 DK

The Nordic nRF52832 (Cortex-M4F, 512 KB flash, 64 KB SRAM, 2.4 GHz radio) is
used here with the nRF52 DK (PCA10040). The board profile maps its four LEDs,
four buttons, and UARTE0 virtual-COM transmit pin. Its runnable smoke is in
[`examples/nrf52-dk`](../../examples/nrf52-dk/ONBOARDING.md).

The current chip descriptor declares multiple nRF52 blocks, including GPIO,
UARTE/UART, clock, timers, RTC, serial buses, SAADC, and radio. The onboarding
smoke makes claims only for the GPIO output and UARTE0 transmit path. It does
not validate the radio, NFC, buttons, serial buses, timers, ADC, or other
declared blocks. See the chip descriptor and the board-local
[`EXTERNAL_COMPONENTS.md`](../../examples/nrf52-dk/EXTERNAL_COMPONENTS.md) for
the modeled boundary.
