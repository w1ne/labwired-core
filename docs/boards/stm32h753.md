# STM32H753ZIT6 coverage

The STM32H753ZIT6 is an active Cortex-M7 MCU with 2 MiB flash and up to 1 MiB
RAM. The demand list puts it in the **other STM32** group, rank 4 at **13%**
reported use. ST currently marks the part active and in volume production.

The working profile is the exact
[`stm32h753zi` descriptor](../../configs/chips/stm32h753zi.yaml), used by the
[NUCLEO-H753ZI board](nucleo_h753zi.md). The profile models the H7 RCC,
GPIOB/C/D, USART3, SysTick, and the declared memory windows. Its firmware smoke
prints a UART marker and toggles LD1 on PB0; the committed result passes 3/3
assertions.

The machine-imported [`configs/chips/onboarding/stm32h753.yaml`](../../configs/chips/onboarding/stm32h753.yaml)
remains a generated descriptor and is not the model used by this profile.
Other H753 peripherals and the complete NUCLEO-H753ZI board are outside the
current model. The smoke provides simulator evidence only; it does not prove
physical pin behavior or a physical-board capture.

See [live vendor status and sources](../../examples/nucleo-h753zi/vendor-status.md),
the [firmware quick start](../../examples/nucleo-h753zi/README.md), and
[smoke evidence](../../examples/nucleo-h753zi/evidence/).
