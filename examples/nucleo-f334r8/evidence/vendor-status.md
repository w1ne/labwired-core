# NUCLEO-F334R8 live vendor status

Checked 2026-11-04 UTC against ST's product pages and online store.

- ST's [NUCLEO-F334R8 product page](https://www.st.com/en/evaluation-tools/nucleo-f334r8)
  lists the board as **Active** and says **“Product is in volume production.”**
- ST's [NUCLEO-F334R8 store listing](https://estore.st.com/en/products/evaluation-tools/product-evaluation-tools/mcu-mpu-eval-tools/stm32-mcu-mpu-eval-tools/stm32-nucleo-boards/nucleo-f334r8.html)
  showed **Active**, **In Stock**, **Add to Cart**, and **$32.89** at the two-unit
  quantity shown when checked.
- ST's [STM32F334R8 product page](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32f334r8.html)
  lists the family as **Active** and in volume production; the board uses the
  STM32F334R8T6.
- ST's [STM32 Nucleo-64 board manual UM1724](https://www.st.com/resource/en/user_manual/um1724-stm32-nucleo64-boards-mb1136-stmicroelectronics.pdf)
  identifies NUCLEO-F334R8 in the MB1136 family, maps PA2/PA3 to USART2 on the
  Arduino D1/D0 pins, and maps the LD2 user LED to the D13 signal. The F334R8
  board pinout maps D13 to PA5.

The availability and price were visible on the linked store page at the time of
the check and can change.
