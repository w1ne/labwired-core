# Live vendor status: NUCLEO-F722ZE

Checked 2026-11-05 UTC against STMicroelectronics' live product page and store.

- ST's [NUCLEO-F722ZE product page](https://www.st.com/en/evaluation-tools/nucleo-f722ze.html)
  reports **Active** and “Product is in volume production.”
- ST's [online store listing](https://estore.st.com/en/products/evaluation-tools/product-evaluation-tools/mcu-mpu-eval-tools/stm32-mcu-mpu-eval-tools/stm32-nucleo-boards/nucleo-f722ze.html)
  showed the board **Active** and **In Stock**, at **$27.65 for the displayed
  two-unit quantity**, with **Add to Cart** available.
- The product page describes this as a Nucleo-144 development board with an
  STM32F722ZE MCU.
- ST's [UM1974 Rev 10 board manual](https://www.st.com/resource/en/user_manual/dm00244518-stm32-nucleo144-boardsstmicroelectronics.pdf)
  maps the green user LED LD1 to PB0 with the default solder-bridge setting and
  says user LEDs turn on when their I/O is high.
- ST's [STM32F722ZE product page](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32f722ze.html)
  identifies the STM32F722ZE line as Active. The [STM32F722xx datasheet,
  DS11853 Rev 9](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32f722ze.pdf)
  specifies 512 KiB Flash for the ZE density and 64 KiB DTCM used for this
  smoke's stack/data region.

The board was Active and directly purchasable from ST at this check. Price and
inventory can change.
