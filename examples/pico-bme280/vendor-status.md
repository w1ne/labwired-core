# Live vendor status — Raspberry Pi Pico + Adafruit BME280 breakout

Checked live on 2026-11-11 UTC. Adafruit's [Raspberry Pi Pico RP2040 product
listing, PID 4864](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4864) listed the unsoldered
Pico at **$4.00**, marked it **In stock**, and showed **Add to Cart**. The
pre-soldered and wireless variants were out of stock; this onboarding targets
the in-stock standard Pico.

Adafruit's [BME280 STEMMA QT breakout listing, PID
2652](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2652) listed the breakout at **$14.95**,
marked it **In stock**, and showed **Add to Cart**. This establishes a buyable
assembled sensor module carrying the BME280; it does not claim that the bare
Bosch LGA sensor was stocked by every distributor.

Raspberry Pi's [Pico product page](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-pico/)
identifies RP2040 and lists the Pico's I2C controllers and GPIO. Bosch's
[BME280 product page](https://www.bosch-sensortec.com/en/products/environmental-sensors/humidity-sensors-bme280)
continues to list BME280 as a current product under its “10 years delivery”
heading and specifies I2C/SPI interfaces; it does not label the part NRND or
obsolete. Bosch's linked distributors all showed zero bare-sensor stock at
this check, so bare-chip availability is not claimed. The exact Adafruit
assembled breakout used in this smoke was in stock and available to order.
