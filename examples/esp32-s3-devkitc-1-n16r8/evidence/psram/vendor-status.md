# ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 N16R8 availability

Checked live on 2026-11-10 for rank-1 ESP32-family maintenance/depth work.

- Espressif's [current ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 v1.1 guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32s3/esp32-s3-devkitc-1/user_guide_v1.1.html)
  lists `ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N16R8` as a current ordering variant with 16 MB
  flash and 8 MB PSRAM. The same live guide marks older N8 and N8R2 variants
  EOL, but does not mark N16R8 EOL.
- Espressif's [live DevKits catalog](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/devkits/esp32-s3-series/esp32-s3-devkitc-1)
  links the exact N16R8 board to Mouser.
- Mouser's [exact N16R8 listing](https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Espressif-Systems/ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N16R8?qs=LnCj936TGbUBJEUUxFQ%252B%252BQ%3D%3D)
  currently identifies it as a new product with factory lead time. It showed
  no on-hand stock when checked; the live order-information panel showed 497
  units on order with expected deliveries on 2026-12-07 and 2026-12-15. It is
  orderable, but immediate shipment was not confirmed.
- Wavtron's live [N16R8 DevKitC listing](https://www.wavtron.in/products/esp32-s3-devkitc-n16r8-dev-board)
  showed **In Stock**, ₹760, and Add to Cart. It identifies 16 MB flash and
  8 MB PSRAM but does not name the board manufacturer or PCB revision, so it
  is evidence for an in-stock matching N16R8 board, not proof that its stock
  is the official v1.1 PCB.

Earlier same-day evidence in the SHT40 receipt showed 77 units at Mouser
before this refresh. This later check is the current Mouser availability
observation for this round.
