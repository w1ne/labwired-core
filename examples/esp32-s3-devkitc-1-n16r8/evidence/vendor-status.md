# Live status and availability check

Checked 2026-11-06 UTC for the ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N16R8 board.

- Espressif's live [ESP32-S3 DevKit catalog](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/devkits/esp32-s3-series/esp32-s3-devkitc-1)
  lists **ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N16R8** and links it to Mouser. The N16R8 is not
  marked EOL or NRND on the catalog page.
- Espressif's current [ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 v1.1 guide](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32s3/esp32-s3-devkitc-1/user_guide_v1.1.html)
  lists N16R8 as an ordering variant. It explicitly labels older N8 and N8R2
  variants EOL; it does not label N16R8 EOL.
- Wavtron's live [N16R8 board listing](https://www.wavtron.in/products/esp32-s3-devkitc-n16r8-dev-board)
  showed **In Stock**, **Add to Cart**, and ₹760 when checked. Its page says it
  was updated on 2026-09-29 and identifies the N16R8 module, but does not state
  which PCB revision will ship.

Demand rank: ESP32 family **#1** in the standing demand evidence (38% reported
MCU use; 42% ESP32 kits). The exact N16R8 board remains listed by Espressif and
was offered in stock by a retailer at the time of this check. The simulation
profile targets v1.1; retailer stock does not distinguish board revisions.
