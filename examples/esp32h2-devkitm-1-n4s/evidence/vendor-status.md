# Live vendor and availability status — ESP32-H2-DevKitM-1-N4S

Checked live on 2026-11-05.

- Espressif's [ESP32-H2-DevKitM-1 guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32h2/esp32-h2-devkitm-1/user_guide.html) lists **ESP32-H2-DevKitM-1-N4S** as the current 4 MiB variant and explicitly marks the older **N4** variant NRND. It documents the onboard addressable RGB LED on GPIO8 and retail order paths. This profile targets N4S only.
- DigiKey's [exact N4S listing](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/espressif-systems/ESP32-H2-DEVKITM-1-N4S/26282483) reports manufacturer part status **Active**, **271 in stock**, and **USD $9.68** per unit when checked.

The board and exact SKU are therefore live and purchasable. The modeled profile covers GPIO8 and the onboard LED only; it does not claim radio, USB, UART bridge, boot, or power behavior.
