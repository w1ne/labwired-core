# Source documents — M5Stack AtomS3-Lite

- M5Stack's [AtomS3-Lite product guide](https://docs.m5stack.com/en/core/AtomS3-Lite)
  identifies SKU C124, ESP32-S3FN8, 8 MB flash, the WS2812C-2020 RGB LED, and
  the exposed connector pins.
- M5Stack's [AtomS3-Lite pinout diagram](https://m5stack.oss-cn-shenzhen.aliyuncs.com/resource/docs/static/pdf/static/en/core/AtomS3%20Lite.pdf)
  labels GPIO35 as RGB, GPIO4 as IR, and GPIO41 as the button.
- The [official M5Stack store page](https://shop.m5stack.com/products/atoms3-lite-esp32s3-dev-kit)
  records current product availability; the dated observation is in
  [`evidence/vendor-status.md`](evidence/vendor-status.md).
- Espressif's [product selector](https://products.espressif.com/product-selector)
  lists the ESP32-S3 SoC as Mass Production.
