# Raspberry Pi Pico 1 lifecycle and availability

Checked live on 2026-11-10 UTC at the official Raspberry Pi product page:

- URL: https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-pico/
- Page title: “Buy a Raspberry Pi Pico”.
- Product page still lists Raspberry Pi Pico 1 and Raspberry Pi Pico W, identifies
  the RP2040 and 16 PWM channels, and provides a local reseller selector under
  “Buy Raspberry Pi Pico 1 series”.
- Obsolescence statement: “Raspberry Pi Pico 1 series will remain in production
  until at least January 2036”.

The board target is the current Raspberry Pi Pico 1 (RP2040, 2 MB flash). The
official lifecycle statement confirms it is not NRND or obsolete.
