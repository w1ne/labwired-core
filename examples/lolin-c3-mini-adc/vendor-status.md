# Live vendor status

Checked 2026-11-11 UTC.

- WEMOS's [LOLIN C3 Mini page](https://docs.wemos.cc/en/latest/c3/c3_mini.html)
  identifies ESP32-C3FH4, lists ADC among the board interfaces, identifies
  V2.1.0 as the latest revision, and links to a **Buy it** listing.
- TinyTronics's live [LOLIN C3 Mini V2.1.0 listing](https://www.tinytronics.nl/en/development-boards/microcontroller-boards/with-wi-fi/wemos-c3-mini-esp32-c3-v2.1.0)
  displayed **200+ pieces in stock**, availability from its Eindhoven
  warehouse, and an **Add to Cart** control.
- Espressif's [current ESP32-C3 datasheet](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-c3_datasheet_en.html)
  lists ESP32-C3FH4 without EOL or NRND status. It separately marks C3FN4 as
  EOL and C3FH4AZ as NRND; neither is the part on this board. The same datasheet
  maps GPIO0 to ADC1_CH0.
- The [WEMOS V2.1.0 schematic](https://docs.wemos.cc/en/latest/_static/files/sch_c3_mini_v2.1.0.pdf)
  is the board-level reference for the header and onboard LED wiring.

These are current vendor and retailer records, not a physical purchase or a
silicon test.
