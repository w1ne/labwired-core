# Live vendor status — Adafruit Feather RP2350 with HSTX

Checked live on 2026-11-04:

- Adafruit's [Feather RP2350 with HSTX product page](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6000),
  product #6000, showed the no-PSRAM option at **$12.50**, **In stock**, and
  **Add to Cart**. This is the selected board variant; the separate version
  with 8 MB PSRAM is not part of this onboarding profile.
- Adafruit's [Feather RP2350 guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-feather-rp2350)
  maps the red indicator LED to GPIO7 and documents `HIGH` as its on state.
  It also maps the onboard NeoPixel to GPIO21 and the STEMMA QT connector to
  I2C1 on GPIO2/GPIO3; those two paths remain outside this smoke profile.

The live manufacturer listing showed the selected board available to purchase.
No archive snapshot was used. Store inventory can change.
