# ESP32-P4X-C5-Function-EV-Board vendor status

Checked live on 2026-11-12.

Espressif's current [ESP DevKits catalog](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/devkits)
lists the `ESP32-P4X-C5-Function-EV-Board` and links to its retail listing. The
current [board guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32p4/esp32-p4x-c5-function-ev-board/user_guide.html)
identifies the exact variant as 16 MB flash / 32 MB PSRAM, gives its ordering
part number, and says retail samples can be ordered through Espressif's
[buy-sample page](https://www.espressif.com/en/company/contact/buy-a-sample).
The guide also identifies this as a P4 v3.x board and gives UART0 on GPIO37/38.
The board is listed as a current development kit, not as NRND or obsolete.

Espressif's current [longevity commitment](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/longevity-commitment)
lists ESP32-P4 for at least 12 years from 2025-01-01. Its [SoC catalog](https://www.espressif.com/products/socs)
lists P4NRW16X and P4NRW32X sample variants with AliExpress links and reference
sample prices. This supports current availability for the main SoC family as
well as current retail availability for the exact development board.
