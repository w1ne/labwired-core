its onboard ST LSM6DSOXTR:

  availability check uses Arduino's in-stock product page above.
- ST's [live LSM6DSOX product page](https://www.st.com/en/mems-and-sensors/lsm6dsox.html)
  identifies LSM6DSOXTR as **Active — product is in volume production**.

  identifies the onboard LSM6DSOXTR and maps it to I2C0 SDA/SCL on GPIO12/GPIO13.
  It maps the exposed TX header to GPIO0 / UART0 TX.

Inventory can change. The profile makes no availability claim for the omitted
