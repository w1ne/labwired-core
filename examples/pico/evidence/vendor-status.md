# Raspberry Pi Pico 1 lifecycle and availability

Checked live on 2026-11-06 at the official Raspberry Pi product page:

- URL: https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-pico/
- Page title: “Buy a Raspberry Pi Pico”
- Availability text: “Powerful, flexible microcontroller boards, available from $4”
- Lifecycle text: “Raspberry Pi Pico 1 series will remain in production until at least January 2036”
- Hardware named on the page: Raspberry Pi Pico with RP2040; the product specification also lists an on-chip temperature sensor.

The live product page qualifies the Pico 1 board for this round's no-obsolete-parts rule.
