# Raspberry Pi Pico and Adafruit BME280 availability

Checked live on 2026-11-10.

- Raspberry Pi's current Pico product page lists the Pico 1 series, built on
  RP2040, as available from **$4** and says the series will remain in
  production until at least **January 2036**. It directs buyers to approved
  resellers. This is an active, purchasable board, not an NRND or obsolete
  part.
- Adafruit's current product page for the exact BME280 STEMMA QT breakout
  (product 2652) lists it **in stock** for **$14.95** with Add to Cart.
