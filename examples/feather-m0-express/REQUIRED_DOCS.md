# Source documents — Feather M0 Express

1. Adafruit's [live product page](https://www.adafruit.com/product/3403) identifies the board and its ATSAMD21G18 MCU.
2. Adafruit's [Feather M0 Express pinout](https://assets-global.website-files.com/5bea6b103705ec65e37d13f6/630d0c0b570951ea7aa558a2_Adafruit%20Feather%20M0%20Express%20pinout.pdf) maps D13 / the red LED to PA17.
3. Adafruit's [board guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-feather-m0-express-designed-for-circuit-python-circuitpython/adafruit2-pinouts) documents the D13 LED, NeoPixel, and 2 MB SPI flash.
4. Adafruit's [downloads page](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-feather-m0-express-designed-for-circuit-python-circuitpython/downloads) links the board diagram and EagleCAD files.
5. Microchip's [live ATSAMD21G18 product page](https://www.microchip.com/en-us/product/ATSAMD21G18) lists its current status and orderable package variants.
6. Microchip [SAM D21/DA1 family datasheet DS40001882](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MCU32/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/SAM-D21-DA1-Family-Data-Sheet-DS40001882.pdf) documents the PM APBBMASK and PORT register maps.
