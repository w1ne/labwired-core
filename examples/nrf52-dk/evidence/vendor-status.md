# nRF52 DK vendor status

Checked **2026-11-03**.

- Nordic's [nRF52 DK product page](https://www.nordicsemi.com/Products/Development-hardware/nRF52-DK/Download)
  still lists the kit for nRF52832 development, identifies its four LEDs and
  four buttons, and publishes hardware files version **3.0.0**. Its hardware
  files changelog records the on-board SoC build code as **nRF52832-QFAAG0**.
- Nordic's [nRF52832 ordering information](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52832/page/ordering_info.html)
  lists the `nRF52832-QFAA-G` order codes without an NRND note. The older
  non-G `nRF52832-QFAA` codes are explicitly marked “Not recommended for new
  designs”; the DK's QFAAG0 build is the production G revision.
- DigiKey's [NRF52-DK listing](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/nordic-semiconductor-asa/nrf52-dk/5773879)
  showed **Active**, **983 in stock**, and an enabled order listing at **$37.32**
  when checked.

The official Nordic product page does not show a separate lifecycle badge for
the kit. The current vendor product/download page and the unflagged G-revision
order codes, together with the distributor's active stock listing, are the
status and purchase evidence for this round.
