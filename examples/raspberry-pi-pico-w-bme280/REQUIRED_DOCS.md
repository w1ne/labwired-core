# Source documents for this profile

- Raspberry Pi's [Pico W product information portal](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/categories/686-raspberry-pi-pico-w) identifies the current board SKU and hosts its datasheet and pinout.
- Raspberry Pi's [Pico W pinout](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/picow/PicoW-A4-Pinout.pdf) is the physical-board reference for the GP4/GP5 wiring.
- Raspberry Pi's [RP2040 datasheet](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rp2040/rp2040-datasheet.pdf) is the peripheral and pad-multiplexing reference.
- Bosch's [BME280 datasheet](https://www.bosch-sensortec.com/media/boschsensortec/downloads/datasheets/bst-bme280-ds001.pdf) is the sensor register and I2C-interface reference.
- Adafruit's [BME280 PID 2652 product page](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2652) identifies the modeled breakout.
- The live buyability check is recorded in [`evidence/vendor-status.md`](evidence/vendor-status.md).
