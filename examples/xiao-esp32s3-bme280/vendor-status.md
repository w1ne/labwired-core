Checked 2026-11-11.

- Seeed's exact standard XIAO ESP32-S3 board (SKU 113991114) was listed for
  **$7.49, In stock**, with an Add to Cart control. The Sense and Plus versions
  are separate products and are not substituted here.

  In Stock**, with an Add to Cart control. Bosch's current product page still

  its current datasheet; the chip-level distributor listing marks the part
  Active.
