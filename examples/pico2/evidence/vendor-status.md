# Raspberry Pi Pico 2 live availability

Checked 2026-11-11 UTC against Raspberry Pi's live product page:

- [Raspberry Pi Pico 2 product page](https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-pico-2/)
  labels it “Available now from $5”, provides a “Buy Raspberry Pi Pico 2”
  reseller path, and says the Pico 2 series will remain in production until at
  least January 2040.
- The product page identifies Pico 2 as an RP2350 board and lists its core,
  memory, and peripheral specifications. It does not mark the board obsolete
  or NRND.

Demand position: **RP2350 family, rank 5** in the measured MCU list (12%); the
demand-ordered target list places the RP2040/RP2350 group at #2. This round
adds a canonical Pico 2 board system and a GP25 LED + UART0 smoke over the
existing RP2350 model; it does not add a new chip model.
