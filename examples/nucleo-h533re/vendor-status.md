This breakout includes a 3.3 V regulator and level shifting. Its default I2C address is `0x77`, per the [Adafruit pinout guide](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-bme280-humidity-barometric-pressure-temperature-sensor-breakout/pinouts).
