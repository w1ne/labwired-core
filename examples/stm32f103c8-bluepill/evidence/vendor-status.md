# STM32F103C8T6 Blue Pill vendor status

Checked live on **2026-11-05**.

- ST's [STM32F103C8 product page](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32f103c8.html)
  lists the family as **Active** and says the product is in volume production.
- ST's [STM32F103C8T6 eStore page](https://estore.st.com/en/stm32f103c8t6-cpn.html)
  identifies the exact MCU and lists it as **Active**. ST's own store currently
  shows it as out of stock, so that page alone is not used as the buyability
  proof.
- LCSC's [STM32F103C8T6 listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/microcontroller-units-mcus-mpus-socs_stmicroelectronics-stm32f103c8t6_C8734.html)
  showed the exact ST part available for immediate shipping with an Add to Cart
  control when checked.
- Sangar Tech's [STM32F103C8T6 Blue Pill board listing](https://www.sangartech.com/products/stm32f103c8t6-blue-pill-development-board)
  showed **$3.00**, **In stock**, and **Add to cart**.

The board profile follows the PC13 LED wiring in the
[Blue Pill schematic](https://stm32-base.org/assets/pdf/boards/original-schematic-STM32F103C8T6-Blue_Pill.pdf).
Blue Pill boards are a generic design with manufacturing variants; this profile
claims only the common STM32F103C8T6 geometry and this schematic's active-low
PC13 LED path.
