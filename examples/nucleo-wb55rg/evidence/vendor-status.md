# Vendor status: NUCLEO-WB55RG

Checked 2026-11-03 against ST's live product and store pages.

- ST's [NUCLEO-WB55RG product page](https://www.st.com/en/evaluation-tools/nucleo-wb55rg.html)
  lists the board as **Active / Product is in volume production** and links to
  direct purchase.
- ST's [online store listing](https://estore.st.com/en/nucleo-wb55rg-cpn.html)
  lists the board as **Active** and **In stock**, shows **Add to cart**, and
  exposes direct purchase.
- ST's [STM32WB55RG product page](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32wb55rg.html)
  lists the MCU as **Active / Product is in volume production**. The ordering
  table lists the STM32WB55RGV6, V6TR, V7, and V7TR order codes as Active.

Pin assignments are based on ST [UM2819](https://www.st.com/resource/en/user_manual/um2819-stm32wb-nucleo64-board-mb1355-stmicroelectronics.pdf)
and the maintained [Zephyr board DTS](https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr/blob/main/boards/st/nucleo_wb55rg/nucleo_wb55rg.dts).
