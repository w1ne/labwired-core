# Live vendor availability — ESP32-C6-DevKitC-1-N8

Checked live on 2026-11-10 UTC:

- Espressif's [current DevKit catalog](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/devkits)
  lists ESP32-C6-DevKitC-1-N8 and current distributor purchase links, including
  DigiKey and Mouser.
- Espressif's [ESP32-C6-DevKitC-1 v1.2 guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32c6/esp32-c6-devkitc-1/user_guide.html)
  lists the N8 variant and explains that the board is available for retail
  orders. It documents the exposed header GPIOs and the onboard GPIO8 RGB LED.

The modeled target is the current ESP32-C6-DevKitC-1-N8 with ESP32-C6-WROOM-1.
The SG90 is an external fixture on GPIO4, not an onboard board component.
