# WCH CH32V003EVT availability and lifecycle

Checked live on 2026-11-13.

- The [Olimex WCH-CH32V003EVT listing](https://www.olimex.com/Products/RISC-V/WCH-CH32V003EVT/)
  showed **In Stock**, **3.95 EUR**, and **Add to basket**. It identifies the
  fitted processor as `CH32V003F4P6`, the USB-C port as power-only, and links
  the board schematic and WCH demo code.
- The [Electrokit WCH-CH32V003EVT listing](https://www.electrokit.com/en/dev-board-for-ch32v003-riscv-processor)
  showed **12 pcs in stock**, **69 SEK**, and **Add to cart**. It identifies
  the same WCH board and `CH32V003F4P6` processor.
- WCH's [CH32V003 product page](https://www.wch-ic.com/products/CH32V003.html)
  and [CH32V00x evaluation-board manual](https://www.olimex.com/Products/RISC-V/WCH-CH32V003EVT/resources/CH32V00x-Evaluation-Board-Reference-EN.pdf)
  remain available with the device and board documentation. WCH does not show
  a lifecycle field on that page, so this receipt makes a current buyability
  claim from the two stocked listings rather than labeling the part “Active.”
- The [board schematic](https://www.olimex.com/Products/RISC-V/WCH-CH32V003EVT/resources/CH32V00xSCH.pdf)
  shows LED1/LED2 on connector P4; this simulator profile records the selected
  P4-1-to-PC0 jumper. Its 1 kΩ resistor and VCC-side LED wiring make LED1
  active low.

This is a Tier C WCH vendor bet from the standing demand brief, not a ranked
survey pick. The evidence supports current purchase availability. It does not
claim the entire CH32V003 peripheral set or physical-board validation.
