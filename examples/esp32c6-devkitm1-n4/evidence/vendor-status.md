# Live availability — ESP32-C6-DevKitM-1-N4

Checked 2026-11-06:

- Espressif's [live product page](https://www.espressif.com/en/dev-board/esp32-c6-devkitm-1-en)
  lists the ESP32-C6-DevKitM-1-N4 and links to DigiKey, Mouser, Amazon, and
  other sellers. It identifies 4 MB flash.
- The [current Espressif user guide](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32c6/esp32-c6-devkitm-1/index.html)
  lists N4 as the available board variant and says its addressable RGB LED is
  driven by GPIO8.
- The [official board schematic](https://dl.espressif.com/dl/schematics/esp32-c6-devkitm-1-schematics.pdf)
  identifies D6 as WS2812B and connects DIN to GPIO8.
- DigiKey's [exact ESP32-C6-DEVKITM-1-N4 listing](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/espressif-systems/ESP32-C6-DEVKITM-1-N4/18667011)
  showed **Part Status: Active**, **273 in stock**, and **US$8.00 each**.

This is a currently orderable board. Availability and price can change after
this check.
