# Live vendor status and availability

Checked 2026-11-06 UTC for Espressif's **ESP32-C3-LCDkit** with its 1.28-inch
LCD daughterboard attached.

- Espressif's live [ESP DevKits catalog](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/devkits/esp32)
  lists the ESP32-C3-LCDkit with the LCD screen, speaker, buttons, encoder and
  IR sensor, and links retailer listings at AliExpress, DigiKey, Mouser,
  Amazon US and Robu India. This is the exact board selection in this profile.
- Espressif's live [ESP32-C3-LCDkit user guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32c3/esp32-c3-lcdkit/user_guide.html)
  identifies the board as an ESP32-C3-MINI-1 design with 4 MB flash, describes
  sample and retail ordering, and gives the LCD daughterboard's GC9A01/SPI/
  240×240 details and GPIO allocation.
- The selected product is the current **ESP32-C3-LCDkit**. It is not the older
  **ESP32-C3-LCD-Ev-Board**, which the same live guide calls an engineering
  sample that is minimally maintained.

The catalog provides purchase links but does not show live stock counts, and
the guide does not label the board lifecycle with an explicit “Active” status.
The evidence here is its current vendor catalog listing and direct retail/sample
order path; no claim is made about stock at any individual retailer.

**Demand rank:** ESP32 family #1 (38% reported MCU use in the standing demand
evidence). This is a board-level display integration, not a new chip.
